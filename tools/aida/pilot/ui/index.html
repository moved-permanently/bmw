Native DA/EDS handoff

  1. Finish the local approval and embargo checks, then download the approved DA HTML.
  2. Open the matching DA editor. Import/paste the document only into an authorized content path. Preview non-confidential samples.
  3. Use native Translate/BYO-AI for language copies. The pilot's rollout is structural copying, not translation.
  4. In DA Snapshots on main, capture the preview, request review, reject/unlock if needed, then have the authorized publisher approve and publish at the allowed time.

Local approvals do not restrict the native EDS Publish button. Real launch protection requires DA permissions, protected EDS preview/origins, publish roles and (if needed) enabled native scheduling. Shared snapshot passwords are not per-person identity.
